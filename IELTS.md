# IELTS 口语练习笔记

## 1. Topic: Travel destinations

I would love to travel to destinations across the globe, both overseas and within my home country. I'm particularly fond of travelling, and places such as Europe, America and East Asia all intrigue me. I'm genuinely fascinated by the lives of people from all corners of the world.

## 2. Topic: Things to experience when travelling abroad

Well, naturally, I'd like to visit well-known local landmarks, as well as natural scenery such as mountains, rivers and seas. However, my favourite thing is to stay with local residents and gain insight into their everyday lives. I'm curious about what they eat on a daily basis, what types of clothing they wear, and their views on the world. I really enjoy having conversations with them in this way.
